# Business Brief

**Client:** Private investor with AED 1.5M to deploy in Dubai residential property.

**Question:** Which area, property type and bedroom count gives the best gross rental yield, with enough market liquidity to exit later?

**Decision supported:** A shortlist of 3–5 segments worth viewing.

**What "good" looks like:**
- Gross yield above the Dubai median
- At least 20 sales and 30 new rent contracts in the last 12 months
- Rents growing at least as fast as prices (no yield compression)

**Data:** Dubai Land Department open data — sales transactions and Ejari rent contracts.
